Privacy Policy

AI Photo to Art

Last Updated: May 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how ssamgr ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use AI Photo to Art. By using the app, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Photos and Text Prompts

To generate artwork, the app sends your selected photo and any text prompt you provide to OpenAI's image generation API. Only the image pixels and text you enter are transmitted. We do not send metadata, location data, or any other personal information from your device.

1.2 Automatically Collected Information

The app may automatically collect:

  • Device information (device type, operating system version)
  • App usage statistics and crash reports
  • Advertising identifiers for personalized ads

1.3 Purchases

Subscription and purchase data are processed by Apple App Store and RevenueCat. We do not collect or store your payment information.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Generate AI artwork via OpenAI's API
  • Provide, maintain, and improve the app
  • Display relevant advertisements
  • Analyze app usage and fix crashes
  • Manage subscriptions and in-app purchases

3. AI Processing and Data Retention

Your photos and prompts are processed by OpenAI. OpenAI does not use API submissions to train its models. We do not store your photos or generated artwork on our servers. All images are processed in real-time and are not retained after generation.

4. Third-Party Services

4.1 OpenAI

AI image generation is powered by OpenAI. Their use of data is governed by OpenAI's Privacy Policy: https://openai.com/privacy/

4.2 Google AdMob

We use Google AdMob to serve advertisements. AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier to provide personalized ads. You can opt out of personalized advertising through your device settings. Learn more: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4.3 RevenueCat

We use RevenueCat to manage subscriptions and in-app purchases. RevenueCat's privacy policy is available at: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/

4.4 Apple Services

Your use of the App Store and iCloud is subject to Apple's Privacy Policy: https://www.apple.com/privacy/

5. Data Storage and Security

Generated artwork and user preferences are stored locally on your device. Photos you select are only sent to OpenAI for processing and are not retained by us. We do not operate backend servers that store user content.

6. Your Rights and Choices

You have the right to:

  • Opt out of personalized advertising in your device settings
  • Request deletion of your data by contacting us
  • Cancel subscriptions through Apple App Store settings

7. Children's Privacy

AI Photo to Art is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
